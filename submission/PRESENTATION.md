## Presentation Highlights
1. **Problem Statement & Background:** High alert fatigue from raw unclassified NASA FIRMS satellite data.
2. **Value Proposition:** Autonomous classification between benign industrial flares, accidental industrial fires, wildfires, and agricultural burning.
3. **Architecture & Multi-Modal Fusion:** Satellite telemetry + OpenStreetMap + ESA WorldCover + WorldPop data ingestion.
4. **Machine Learning Pipeline:** XGBoost 14-feature classifier, explainability cards, and operational risk ranking.
5. **Interactive Demonstration:** Live GIS command centre, 3D WebGL globe, priority triage queue, and incident investigator.
6. **Future Roadmap:** Ground sensor integration, drone dispatch triggers, and national emergency agency integration (NDRF, SDMA).
