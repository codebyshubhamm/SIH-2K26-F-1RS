## Demonstration Walkthrough Structure
1. **Executive Overview (0:00 – 0:45):**
   - Highlighting the challenge of raw NASA FIRMS coordinates.
   - Introducing THERMOS and its 3D thermal anomaly earth visualization.
2. **Tactical Command Centre & GIS Map (0:45 – 1:45):**
   - Live streaming of thermal events across industrial corridors in India.
   - Interactive inspection of heat clusters, industrial perimeter polygons, and popups.
3. **Automated Priority Queue & Triage (1:45 – 2:30):**
   - Sorting by Operational Risk Score (Critical, High, Moderate, Low).
   - Filtering by confidence threshold, date ranges, and event categories.
4. **AI Incident Investigator (2:30 – 3:30):**
   - Deep-dive into an anomaly evidence card: persistence hours, FRP spike, land-use confirmation.
   - Human-in-the-loop operator review and validation workflow.
5. **System Health & Ingestion Pipeline (3:30 – 4:00):**
   - Monitoring API latency, pipeline throughput, and backend telemetry health.
